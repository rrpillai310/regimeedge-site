The Regime Classifier

Most bots use MA crossovers and blow up in volatile markets. RegimeEdge labels every bar before deciding whether to trade. STRESS blocks all entries. No exceptions.

📈

TREND

Directional momentum confirmed. Full position sizing. Once profitable, a breakeven stop activates — a winning trade can never turn into a loss.

→ Full size  ·  All signals active
↔

RANGE

Sideways chop detected. Position size automatically halved. Signals still fire but with tighter risk controls and a higher score threshold.

→ Half size  ·  Tighter stops
⚠

STRESS

Elevated volatility. Direction unclear. All new buys blocked regardless of signal strength. This single feature prevents the majority of drawdowns.

→ No new positions  ·  Cash preserving
